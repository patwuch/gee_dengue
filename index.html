Spatiotemporal Dengue Risk Prediction in Southeast Asia and Taiwan

A two-pronged deep learning early warning system operating at province and sub-district scale

Last updated: —  ·  Forecast month: —  ·  SEA regions covered: 0

Study Overview

This project combines two complementary prediction models targeting different geographic scales and use cases:

Arm 1 — Southeast Asia Provinces

A spatiotemporally-aware deep learning model (ST-GAT + GRU) trained on 162 admin-1 provinces across 10 SEA countries (2011–2018). It captures international dengue risk dynamics using province-level historical infection data, monthly climatological data (ERA5-Land, MODIS, CHIRPS), and Shared Socioeconomic Pathways. Inference runs monthly and is bias-corrected to account for post-2018 climate shift (AR6 SSP2-4.5).

View SEA Provinces Results →

Arm 2 — Taiwan Basic Statistics Area (BSA)

A Taiwan-specific counterpart operating at the finer granularity of the Basic Statistics Area (BSA) — the smallest census unit tracked by the Taiwanese government. It jointly considers international mobility (using SEA risk states from Arm 1 as exporter signals), local mobility, BSA-level environmental suitability, and population demographics to predict elevated local dengue risk.

About

Dengue fever’s transmission dynamics in Southeast Asia relies on human and vector mobility, displaying a high degree of non-linearity and unpredictability. Moreover, as dengue outbreaks in Taiwan are attributed to imported cases from Southeast Asia coinciding with windows of time where weather favors vector reproductability, preventative measures must be taken at the right time in the right places. To address the limits of considering dengue outbreak in isolation which ignores how circulating dengue serotypes confer patterns that may only be explained across multiple countries extended beyond neighboring years, this study creates a two-pronged experimental design that combines: (1) a Southeast Asian spatiotemporally-aware deep learning prediction model that captures international dengue risk dynamics based on province-level historical infection data, near-real-time climatological data, as well as Shared Socioeconomic Pathways data. (2) a Taiwan-only counterpart whose prediction works at a finer granularity, a Basic Statistics Area (BSA), and predicts based on individual case report, clinical diagnosis, demographic data, and environmental data.

SEA Provinces Pipeline

  1. Monthly zonal statistics fetched from Google Earth Engine
  2. Climate delta correction applied (AR6 SSP2-4.5) to adjust for post-2018 climate shift
  3. STGNN inference run on adjusted features
  4. Results published automatically via GitHub Actions

Architecture: ST-GAT + GRU (PyTorch Geometric) Training period: 2011–2018 Features: ERA5-Land, MODIS LST, NDVI/EVI, CHIRPS, LULC, WorldPop, SSP population Spatial scope: 162 admin-1 regions across 10 SEA countries Performance (test set): Huber 0.390 · MAE 1.214 · RMSE 2.460 (log IR, 14 held-out months)

Taiwan BSA Pipeline

  1. SEA province risk states ingested as international mobility proxy (from Arm 1)
  2. BSA-level environmental suitability computed from TCCIP climate data
  3. Individual case reports and demographic data from Taiwan Ministry of Health
  4. BSA model inference generates sub-district risk predictions

Architecture: (to be finalised) Training period: (to be determined) Features: MoH case reports, clinical diagnosis, demographics, TCCIP climate, local mobility Spatial scope: Taiwan Basic Statistics Areas (BSAs) Performance: (pending model training)

Interpretation note: SEA predictions are expressed as a relative risk index (predicted log IR normalised to the 2011–2018 baseline), not absolute case counts. This accounts for structural model uncertainty arising from the 8-year gap between training data and current conditions.

## SEA Provinces — Current Risk Map

Predicted dengue incidence rate (relative risk index) for admin-1 regions across Southeast Asia.
Values > 1 indicate elevated risk relative to the 2011–2018 training baseline.

```{python}
#| echo: false
#| label: fig-risk-map
#| fig-cap: "Predicted relative dengue risk index. Delta bias correction applied to account for 2018–2026 climate shift (AR6 SSP2-4.5)."

import json, pathlib
import plotly.express as px
import pandas as pd

data_dir = pathlib.Path("data")
preds_path = data_dir / "latest_predictions.json"

if not preds_path.exists():
    print("⚠️  No prediction data found — run site/scripts/run_inference.py first.")
else:
    preds = json.loads(preds_path.read_text())
    regions = preds.get("regions", {})
    df = pd.DataFrame([
        {"region": k, "country": v["country"], "risk_index": v["risk_index"]}
        for k, v in regions.items()
    ])

    fig = px.choropleth(
        df,
        locations="country",
        locationmode="country names",
        color="risk_index",
        hover_name="region",
        hover_data={"risk_index": ":.2f", "country": True},
        color_continuous_scale="RdYlGn_r",
        range_color=[0, 3],
        labels={"risk_index": "Risk Index"},
        title=f"Dengue Risk Index — {preds.get('target_month', '')}",
        scope="asia",
    )
    fig.update_layout(margin=dict(l=0, r=0, t=40, b=0), height=480)
    fig.show()
```

## SEA Provinces — Risk Index Trends

Mean risk index across all SEA admin-1 regions over every month predictions have been archived.

```{python}
#| echo: false
#| label: fig-trends
#| fig-cap: "Monthly mean relative risk index across all SEA admin-1 regions. Values > 1 indicate elevated risk vs the 2011–2018 baseline."

import json, pathlib
import plotly.graph_objects as go

archive_dir = pathlib.Path("data/predictions")
records = []
for f in sorted(archive_dir.glob("*.json")):
    data = json.loads(f.read_text())
    regions = data.get("regions", {})
    valid = [r["risk_index"] for r in regions.values() if r.get("has_data") and r.get("risk_index") is not None]
    if valid:
        records.append({
            "month": data["target_month"],
            "mean_risk": round(sum(valid) / len(valid), 4),
            "n_regions": len(valid),
        })

if not records:
    print("No archived predictions yet — will populate after the first monthly run.")
else:
    months = [r["month"] for r in records]
    means  = [r["mean_risk"] for r in records]

    fig = go.Figure()
    fig.add_trace(go.Scatter(
        x=months, y=means, mode="lines+markers",
        line=dict(color="#e63946", width=2),
        marker=dict(size=6),
        name="Mean risk index",
        hovertemplate="%{x}<br>Risk index: %{y:.3f}<extra></extra>",
    ))
    fig.add_hline(y=1.0, line_dash="dash", line_color="grey",
                  annotation_text="Baseline (2011–2018 mean)", annotation_position="bottom right")
    fig.update_layout(
        xaxis_title="Month", yaxis_title="Mean Risk Index",
        height=320, margin=dict(l=0, r=0, t=20, b=0),
    )
    fig.show()
```
